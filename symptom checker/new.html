Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Symptom Checker?

A Symptom Checker is an interactive tool that helps users identify potential health conditions based on their symptoms. It provides a list of possibilities but is not a substitute for a professional diagnosis.

How accurate is the Symptom Checker?

Our tool provides a data-driven likelihood of conditions but should always be followed by a consultation with a healthcare professional for an accurate diagnosis.

Is my data safe?

Yes, your privacy is our priority. We do not store any personal health information entered into the Symptom Checker. All data is anonymized.
